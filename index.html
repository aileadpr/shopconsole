FAQ

What games can I play?

It runs Android, so you can install games from the Play Store, use cloud gaming and streaming apps, and play your own compatible game library. The physical controls work with any game that supports a controller.

What is the second screen for?

It's a touch screen you can use for maps, inventory, menus, a walkthrough or a chat while the game stays full-screen on top. It also shows FPS, temperature and fan speed, and gives one-tap access to performance modes and control settings.

Will the joysticks drift?

No. The Hall-effect joysticks use magnets instead of physical contacts, so they don't wear out and don't develop the drift common on traditional sticks.

Can I connect it to Wi-Fi and Bluetooth?

Yes. It has Wi-Fi for downloads and streaming and Bluetooth for headphones and controllers.

How do I charge it?
